# lemon-x-files

The multi-transport generator corpus: one surface spec, seven transports — REST, GraphQL, SSE,
JSON-RPC, UDP, Socket.IO and OTLP — declared as modules in the same surface rather than forks.

This is the working record behind [the last five years](/ideas/generator-notes). It is not a
portfolio piece; it is what broke while I was trying to make one generator honest. The gaps are
written down alongside the transport modules, which is the only reason the record is useful to
anyone else.

Repository: [gibsunas/lemon-x-files](https://github.com/gibsunas/lemon-x-files).
