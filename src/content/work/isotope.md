# isotope

A trust protocol library. An isotope is a named procedure with its slots — the values it cannot
guess — written down so a third party can re-derive the answer from published coordinates.

The problem it answers: a claim that travels without the assumptions it was made under is not
reusable. isotope publishes the shape and the presumptions together, so two systems on different
eras are not silently treated as equivalent.

Repository: [james-gibson/isotope](https://github.com/james-gibson/isotope).
