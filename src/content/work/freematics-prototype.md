# freematics-prototype

My car writes blog posts. Trusting the narrator is the hard part.

The problem it answers: telemetry is a first-person account from a machine. It can be wrong in
ways that look perfectly well-formed, so the record has to carry enough provenance for a reader to
tell a measurement from a story. It is
[checkable claims](/ideas/checkable-claims) applied to a narrator with no interest in being right.
