# adhd

Alarm Dashboard Health Monitor. It collects health state from the lab's surfaces and shows what
is detecting, what has lit, and which surfaces are dark.

The problem it answers: a monitor that only reports "up" cannot tell you whether the detector is
working. adhd keeps the detector, the alarm and the display as separate things so a dark light
means something specific.

Repository: [james-gibson/adhd](https://github.com/james-gibson/adhd).
