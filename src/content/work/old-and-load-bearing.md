# Old and load-bearing

These are not current projects. They are early public work that other things still point at, kept
here because the record should be complete.

- [lightning-bower-example](https://github.com/james-gibson/lightning-bower-example) — a Bower
  example from the Lightning era.
- [forceTooling](https://github.com/james-gibson/forceTooling) — Salesforce development tooling.
- [resumeAPI](https://github.com/james-gibson/resumeAPI) — a resume served as an API.
- [agrig8](https://github.com/james-gibson/agrig8) — the warehouse-routing experiment described in
  [experience](/about/experience).

My first OSS contribution — a
[code archive post on mapping multiple addresses in Google Maps](https://forum.alphasoftware.com/forum/other-forums/code-archive/52839-how-to-map-multiple-addresses-in-google-maps) —
stayed relevant for
[years](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2WnpMWYuLSE). That is the useful measure of early work:
someone was still reading it long after I wrote it.
