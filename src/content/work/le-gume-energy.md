# le.gume-energy

A non-identifying, local-time record of a home solar system: production against weather, with the
residuals separating snow from soiling.

The argument is the accounting. A production number without the conditions it was measured under
is a scoreboard; a record that publishes what was excluded and what still narrows the population
is something a reader can check. The current series covers 1,188 days, and it is the concrete
example behind [environmental sustainability](/about/values).
