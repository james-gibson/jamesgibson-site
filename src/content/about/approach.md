# Approach

## Technical leadership

When the constraints are known — or when they are not — I use data-driven methods to identify
present and future challenges, then effect change at the micro-technical level across an
organisation. The small change and the organisational change are the same job viewed from two
distances.

## Self-service developer enablement

Making it easy for engineers to ship code. Systems that do not frustrate engineers at the micro
level correlate, in my experience, with project success at the macro level. That is a correlation,
not a law: the point is to measure both ends rather than assume the connection.

## Architecture

People architecture. Conway's law is a tool rather than a limitation. Handoffs should be clear and
precise, and the people on both sides of a handoff should be respected during the process.
