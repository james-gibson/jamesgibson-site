# James Gibson

I'm James, a Node.js Platform Architect living in Denver, Colorado. I work remotely, and have
since moving here from the DC metro area in August 2013. Self-taught, community-raised.

**I make claims checkable.** Every system I have observed appears to fail the same way: one part
goes missing, the output stays plausible, and nobody notices. Energy reporting, deployment
pipelines, brokerage accounting, compliance limits, agent-facing services — more than a decade,
one failure shape. The work is about making the missing part visible before the plausible output
is mistaken for an answer.

The CV records 26 years of programming, 12 years working alongside technical, operational and
engineering leadership, and 10 years in hands-on team and project leadership roles (as of
2026-11-19, from the CV copy carried over to this site).
