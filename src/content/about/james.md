# James Gibson

I'm James, a Node.js Platform Architect living in Denver, Colorado. I work remotely, and I have
since moving here from the DC metro area in August 2013.

The CV records 26 years of programming, 12 years working alongside technical, operational and
engineering leadership, and 10 years in hands-on team and project leadership roles (as of
2026-11-19, from the CV copy carried over to this site).

## What that means in practice

Most of my work sits at the boundary between the platform and the people using it. A system that
is technically correct but frustrating to use day to day does not stay correct for long — someone
works around it, and the workaround becomes the system. So I pay attention to the handoff as much
as to the architecture behind it.

The three things I am asked about most are in [approach](/about/approach): technical leadership,
developer enablement, and architecture. The jobs and projects are in
[experience](/about/experience) and [work](/work).
